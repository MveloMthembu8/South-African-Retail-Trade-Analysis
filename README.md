**South African Retail Trade Sales Analysis**



**Project Overview**



This project analyses monthly retail trade sales in South Africa using data published by Statistics South Africa (Stats SA), covering January 2002 to June 2026.



The objective is to identify long term sales trends, seasonal patterns and year-on-year changes to demonstrate how data analysis can support business understanding and decision making.



**Business Questions**



• How have South African retail sales changed over time?

• Which months typically record the highest and lowest sales?

• How has year-on-year sales growth changed?

• How should incomplete annual data be handled when comparing years?



**Tools Used**



• Microsoft Excel: Data preparation, cleaning and exploratory analysis.

• PostgreSQL: SQL queries, aggregations, year-on-year comparisons and data quality checks.

• Microsoft Power BI: Interactive dashboard, KPI cards, time-series analysis and visualization.



**Data**



The dataset contains monthly retail trade sales published by Stats SA, from January 2002 to June 2026. The analysis focuses on total retail trade sales at constant prices, with values reported in millions of South African rand.



The 2026 data covers January to June only and should not be interpreted as a complete annual total.



**Key Findings**



• December has the highest average monthly sales, while January has the lowest, indicating a seasonal pattern.

• Annual growth reached approximately 11.92% in 2006.

• Annual growth fell to approximately -6.58% in 2020.

• Year-on-year growth varied considerably over time highlighting the importance of examining individual periods as well as long term trends.



**Dashboard**



The Power BI dashboard includes:



• Long-term retail sales trends.

• Average monthly sales by calendar month.

• Annual sales comparisons.

• An interactive date slicer.

• A year-on-year growth KPI that responds to the selected period.



**Data Quality**



SQL checks were used to assess missing months, duplicate monthly records, and missing sales values. The checks found no missing months, duplicate months or missing sales values in the prepared analysis dataset.



**Limitations**



• The analysis is descriptive and does not establish the causes of observed changes.

• The 2026 reporting period is incomplete, covering January to June.

• Seasonal patterns and annual changes should be interpreted in the context of the selected sales measure and the period analyzed.
